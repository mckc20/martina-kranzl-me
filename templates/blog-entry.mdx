Start writing here. Text wraps around the photograph on larger screens. Change
`align` to `left` or `center`, and adjust `width` to scale it. Use a percentage
such as `40%`, or a pixel number such as `width={280}`. On a narrow screen the
image automatically becomes full width above the text.

To place a heading beside a photo too, add `wrapHeading` to the `PostImage`
and put the heading immediately after it:

```mdx
<PostImage src={eveningPhoto} alt="Evening sky" align="right" width="40%" wrapHeading />

## A heading beside the photo

The paragraph continues beside the photo as well.
```

Keep writing in ordinary Markdown, including **emphasis**, lists, quotations
and footnotes.[^1] The introduction, excerpt, tags, cover and body photographs
are optional. Remove the example image and its imports if you only want text.
