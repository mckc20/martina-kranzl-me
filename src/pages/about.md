I'm a 14-year-old high school student attending a chemistry-focused school in Vienna. <br>
Here are some things I like...

#### Books
I love reading! My favourite genre is fantasy, and my favourite author is Brandon Sanderson. I highly recommend reading his books if you like fantasy and epic novels. I also recommend them if you don't like fantasy, because he also writes science fiction (so you've got no excuse). <br>
My favourite book is, of course, one of his, although I can't decide *which* one. So I'll just say my favourite series, ["The Stormlight Archive"](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/the-stormlight-archive-series) (It's really long, but totally worth it.)

I listen to music quite often. However, I'm not the kind of person walking around on the street with headphones on. My music taste is not really up to date; it kind of got stuck in the 80s and 90s, but some newer songs are among my favourites too.<br>
My current favourites are ["Tillbaka" by Bo Kaspers Orkester](https://open.spotify.com/intl-de/track/7bcJpQd84fTYEKmkOi8WpA?si=e691f75593f34da6) and ["All Through the Night" by Cyndi Lauper](https://open.spotify.com/intl-de/track/2CIDqYfIYfl9vx02wIyCG9?si=8a6072946b354bf2).<br>
