Not every photograph needs a special occasion. Sometimes a quiet scene at home
is enough: a dog stretched out on the floor, a patch of light, the contrast between
colourful fabric and black-and-white tiles.

This is where you could write about what caught your eye, how you made the
photograph, or what the moment means to you. There is room for a story beyond
the image: the sounds around you, a thought that stayed with you, or a small
detail you might otherwise have forgotten.

The photograph beside these paragraphs is part of the text's flow. On a wider
screen the writing wraps around it, leaving the image close to the words it
belongs with. On a phone it sits above the text so neither has to squeeze
into a narrow column.

This is still an example post: replace the paragraphs with your own writing.
You can make the image wider or narrower, move it to the left, or let it occupy
a line of its own. The photograph keeps its natural proportions as its width
changes.
